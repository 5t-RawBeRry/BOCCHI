# 4.2.0.0

### Main window
- Status bar at the top shows what’s running
- Click a chip to jump to that section
- Mode pages focus on controls, with less repeated status text

### Look & feel
- Gold section titles, card panels, and status chips in Main and Config
- Progress numbers sit beside bars so they’re easier to read

### Auto-shop
- New Shopping page: keep stock, buy a set count, or spend leftover currency
- When silver or gold hits your threshold, shops at camp, then continues
- Pauses other automation while shopping
- Won’t interrupt a FATE, CE, or an active Mob Farmer pull/fight
- Pick which currency to use when an item has more than one
- South Horn uses pieces; North Horn uses obols
- Skips already-owned unlocks and armor

### Shared maps
- Under Config → Treasure Hunter → Share maps with the community
- Downloads community carrot and coffer spots, plus pot timers
- Anonymously uploads what you find
- Turn off for built-in maps and local pot timing only
