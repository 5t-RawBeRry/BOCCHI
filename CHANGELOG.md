# 4.1.0.1

### Fixes
- Mob Farmer tank pulls use the right ranged skill and gap closer (Tomahawk / Onslaught, and the other tanks' equivalents) and send them at the intended enemy. Fight rotation stays off while gathering, so the target should not flicker back onto enemies already on you.
- Critical Encounter wait points use the real registration center and stand further inside the ring, so CEs like Doubled Trouble no longer park on the ramp just outside the circle.
- Wrath, Rotation Solver, and BossMod / BMR no longer spam chat (including "Henched - No Casting") or restart dodges when you enter a FATE or Critical Encounter.
